# Code of conduct

kinemo follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the
work. Harassment and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions
or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through the contact listed on the
repository's profile. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainers may warn,
suspend or ban anyone who breaks these rules.
